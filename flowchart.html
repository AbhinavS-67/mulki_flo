The problem

Mulki floods every monsoon

The Shambhavi rises and the Arabian Sea pushes back. Roads, power and homes were not designed for 3,500 mm of rain a year. Fixes tend to be one-off and unpriced, with no shared baseline data.

Our venture

An asset-light sustainability consultancy

Sustainable Mulki Solutions is a project-design and coordination company. We start with baseline studies, project preparation, feasibility, planning and reporting, working with qualified partners.

We do not assume we will build. Construction and certified engineering stay with specialists.

Mulki is our demonstration method. It is not an approved plan or a secured contract.

The operating model

Assess → Design → Coordinate → Monitor

Every assignment follows the same four steps, so each client gets a clear scope and clear deliverables.

1

Assess

Know the starting point
  • Baseline sustainability audits
  • Energy use and rooftop-solar feasibility
  • Water audits and rainwater feasibility
  • Solid-waste surveys
  • GIS mapping and asset inventory
  • Drainage and waterlogging surveys
2

Design

Turn findings into a plan
  • Town development and investment plans
  • Feasibility studies
  • Costing and phasing
  • Procurement documents
  • Vendor comparisons
3

Coordinate

Get it delivered
  • Implementation coordination
  • Specialist partners: energy, water, GIS, civil and drainage
  • Funding and grant proposals where appropriate
4

Monitor

Prove it worked
  • Energy, water and waste dashboards
  • Annual performance monitoring
  • Workshops and training

Monitoring data feeds the next Assess step, so each cycle starts from measured results.

10
Guardrails

What we promise, and what we don't

  • Use qualified specialists for certified audits, electrical, structural, flood and hydraulic engineering, and signed designs.
  • Never guarantee savings, grants, subsidies, flood prevention or approvals.
  • Disclose any referral fees. Agree written scopes. Protect client data.
  • Follow procurement rules, and do not claim an award without an award record.
